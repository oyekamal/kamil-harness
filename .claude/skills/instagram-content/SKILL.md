# Instagram Content — Reels, Stories & Posts

## Core Principle

**Hook-Substance-Payoff: the only structure that works.**
- Hook (5-7s): Scroll stopper. Opens a loop of curiosity.
- Substance (75-80%): Delivers the value — educate OR entertain (ideally both).
- Payoff: Fulfills the hook's promise. The reason people save and share.

## Phase 1: Hook Library (First 5-7 Seconds)

The hook must **ask a question in the viewer's mind** they need the video to answer.

**Shocking/Surprising:**
- "I can't believe I found this..."
- "This seems illegal to know: ___"
- "Nobody told me that ___"

**Stop action:**
- "Stop doing X"
- "Stop wasting money on ___"
- "Stop believing this myth about ___"

**Time/money savings:**
- "This tip will save you hours"
- "5 minutes that will change ___"

**Questions:**
- "Do you know why ___?"
- "What would you do if ___?"

**Before/After:**
- "Here's what it looked like before... and after"
- "From ___ to ___ in ___ days"

**Mistakes:**
- "5 mistakes you're making with ___"
- "What nobody tells you about ___"

**POV:**
- "POV: You just discovered that ___"
- "POV: You're trying to ___ and this happens"

**Rules for hooks:**
- Under 5 seconds. Simple, direct language. No jargon.
- Combine visual + audio hook for best performance.
- Never use a hook you can't fulfill in the payoff.

## Phase 2: Substance (75-80% of video)

**Goal:** Keep viewers in frame as long as possible (watch time is the key metric).

**Editing techniques:**
- Jump cuts every 3-5 seconds
- Text overlays for key points
- Sound effects at key moments
- B-roll cutaways
- Pattern interrupts (change angle, add graphic)

**Content formats that work:**
- Social conversations / Q&A
- Educational how-to (3-5 numbered points)
- POV skits
- Before/after transformations
- Day-in-the-life behind-the-scenes
- Skill/talent demonstrations
- Challenges / experiments

**Edutainment rule:** If you can make them laugh AND learn — you win.

## Phase 3: Payoff (Last 5-10 seconds)

- **Must fulfill the hook's promise exactly.** If you promised "3 ways to X", deliver all 3.
- End the video **immediately** after the payoff. No dead air.
- Payoff is what makes people **share or save** — design it for that.

## Content Idea Scoring (4 Criteria)

| Criteria | Test |
|---|---|
| Relatable | Does this apply to a large group? |
| Provokes questions | "How did they do that?" "What does that mean?" |
| Provokes reactions | Laugh, surprise, awe, emotion |
| Seems unrealistic | "That can't be true..." → they watch to find out |

Score 3/4 minimum before filming. Quality of idea > quality of production.

## Engagement Tactics (Use At Least 2 Per Video)

**1. Mild controversy:**
Express a genuine controversial opinion on a LIGHT topic.
Why: Forces viewers to pick a side and comment.
Example: "Espresso in the morning is overrated — here's why."
NOT on: religion, human rights, politics.

**2. Intentional mistake:**
Deliberate small error (typo, mispronunciation) that doesn't change the information.
Why: People love pointing out mistakes.

**3. Specific CTA:**
Ask for a specific action.
- ❌ "Like and share" — too generic
- ✅ "Which of these 3 methods do you use? Tell me in comments"
- ✅ "Tag someone who needs to see this"
- ✅ "Save this so you don't forget"

## Caption Copywriting

**Structure:**
1. Hook (first line — stops scroll even before they watch)
2. Context/Story — expand on video's theme
3. Value/Lesson — one takeaway
4. CTA — specific action
5. 5-10 hashtags (at end or first comment)

**Rules:**
- Concrete over vague: "3 things I learned in 5 years as X" not "great tips"
- Emotion over logic: "Imagine having 2 extra hours every day..." not "this process speeds up work"
- No clichés: "quality content", "follow for more" = instant unfollow energy
- Write how you talk. Short sentences. Emoji for tone (not spam).

**Hashtag strategy:**
- 1-2 large (100k+ posts)
- 3-4 medium (10k-100k)
- 2-3 small (<10k)
- 1 branded hashtag

## Technical Settings

**iPhone:**
- 4K, 30fps, HDR off, Auto FPS off, Lock White Balance on
- Format: Most Compatible

**Android:**
- 4K, 30fps, stabilization on, grid on

**Shooting:**
- Angle slightly above eye level (flattering)
- Rule of thirds — eyes on upper grid line
- Natural window light when possible (more light = algorithm favors it)
- Clean lens before every shoot
- Prepare a shot list before filming

## Format Reference

| Element | Reel | Story | Carousel Post |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goal | Viral reach | Engagement with fans | Depth + saves |
| Length | 15-90s | 15s each | 1-10 slides |
| Hook | Critical (5s) | Quick (2-3s) | First slide |
| CTA | In caption | Sticker/Poll | Caption + last slide |
| Hashtags | 5-10 | 1-3 | 5-10 |

## Community Building (10-15 min/day)

- Reply to first comments within 1 hour of posting
- Like all comments
- Ask follow-up questions back ("And you? What's your experience?")
- Use emoji for personal tone
- Loyalty = trust = algorithm signal

## Series Strategy

Pick a format and commit to **7-10 videos** before deciding if it works. The algorithm needs time to recognize your content. Instead of changing format when it's not working, improve the **execution**: better hook, stronger idea, faster pacing.

Series (e.g., "100-Day Challenge", "Learning X in Y days") are highly effective — they give people a reason to come back.
